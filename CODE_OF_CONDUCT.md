# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Give and accept constructive, evidence-based feedback. In this project,
  disagreements are settled with tests and measurements.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its community.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and trolling.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Posting copyrighted game assets, cracked binaries or pirated material.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report incidents privately to the maintainers through
[GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/cotizcesar/starcraft-re-recomp/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting a maintainer through their GitHub profile. All reports are
reviewed promptly and confidentially. Maintainers may remove content and
warn, suspend or ban participants, following the Contributor Covenant
enforcement guidelines.
